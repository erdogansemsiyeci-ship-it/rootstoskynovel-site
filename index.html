The Book

Roots to Sky

By any measure, Elena has escaped: the airless childhood house, the mother whose love arrived as accusation, the old country no one would name. None of her answers explain why a woman who commands any room goes cold at the touch of the man she trusts.

When Julian stops waiting on the other side of the table, Elena does what she has avoided all her life: she goes east. From the wet stone and ferry smoke of Istanbul to a half-empty village in the Black Sea mountains, down to a cove on the Aegean where the water goes quiet, she follows the fear back to the woman it was born in — before the same silence reaches the lives that come after her.

Roots to Sky is a novel of salt, coffee, and what the soul remembers when the mind refuses — a woman putting down a weight she carried so long she stopped naming it.

Author

Erdoğan Şemsiyeci

Erdoğan Şemsiyeci is a psychological counselor and family constellation practitioner who lives and works in Turkey. For more than twenty years he has accompanied people as they trace the quiet inheritances that shape their lives. He is the author of SAT Metot, a book on family systems, has translated some twenty books, and has edited nearly a hundred. Roots to Sky, his first novel, grows from that same ground: what a family carries, what it hands down, and what it takes to meet it rather than repeat it.
