The Book

Elena has spent her whole life perfecting a woman the world admires and cannot afford to be.

Poised, admired, composed, Elena goes cold at her partner’s touch and has never known why. When Julian leaves for the east, she follows him: through Istanbul, to a village on the Black Sea, to a cove on the Aegean.

Each place asks her to look closer at the fear she has organised her life around, until she traces it to its origin, in an old country no one would name.

A novel of salt, coffee, and what the soul remembers when the mind refuses.

The Author

Erdoğan Şemsiyeci

A psychological counselor and family constellation practitioner in Turkey, with more than twenty years of practice. His own family came through migrations from Romania, now Bulgaria, and from Georgia.

The novel grew from a question that kept returning in his work: what do you do when you discover that the ancestor you grieved had caused harm?

He is also the author of SAT Metot, a book on family systems.
